# Task A5 – Design When the Full Model Is Too Expensive (4 Marks)

Choose one edge-detection case: intrusion, emotion, deepfake or disaster detection.

## A5.1 – Full-Model Perspective (1 mark)
Estimate the full model's parameters, memory and operations per sample, and state why it exceeds the 50 ms latency budget.

## A5.2 – Design the Compressed Pipeline (2 marks)
Design an approximation, such as pruning, quantisation, a smaller architecture, feature reduction or an early-exit cascade. Give inference pseudocode and state which operations are saved.

## A5.3 – What Have We Sacrificed? (1 mark)
Identify precisely what accuracy, robustness or explainability may be lost and how to detect it.

**Evidence expected:** Resource estimate, compressed-pipeline pseudocode and trade-off analysis.
