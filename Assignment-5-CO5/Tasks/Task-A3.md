# Task A3 – Discover the Expert-System Architecture Pattern (5 Marks)

Three systems:
1. Pump-fault diagnosis advisor (symptoms → fault).
2. Crop-disease advisor (observations → disease → treatment).
3. Flood-relief triage advisor (reports → priority).

## A3.1 – Identify the Common Pattern (2 marks)
Write at least four rules for each of the three domains. Identify what is common to all three and what is domain-specific. State which parts can be reused without changing code.

## A3.2 – Design a Generic Expert-System Shell (2 marks)
Specify the knowledge base, working memory, inference engine, explanation facility and knowledge-acquisition interface; identify the data each exchanges and give inference-cycle pseudocode including conflict resolution.

## A3.3 – Explain Explanation and Knowledge Acquisition (1 mark)
Explain why HOW and WHY explanations and a knowledge-acquisition interface are essential for domain experts who are not programmers.

**Evidence expected:** Three rule sets, common-versus-specific analysis, shell design with pseudocode and explanation/acquisition reasoning.
